Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Bot collects, how it is used, and how it is handled in compliance with Discord’s Developer Policies and applicable privacy laws (including GDPR).

2. Data We Collect

  • User Information: Discord user IDs, usernames, and avatars (for welcome images).
  • Message Logs: Message content, edits, and deletions (for moderation and logging).
  • Voice Activity Logs: Join, leave, and move events in voice channels.
  • Reports and Tickets: Information submitted by users through the Bot’s reporting and support systems.
  • Timestamps and metadata related to actions performed by users or moderators.

3. How We Use the Data

  • Moderation and server management
  • Maintaining logs accessible to the server’s staff
  • Generating welcome images
  • Supporting tickets and report handling
  • Internal debugging and safety monitoring

4. Data Storage and Access

All data is stored within Discord or via messages accessible to server staff. We do not use external databases or share data with third parties. Access is restricted to authorized members of the server’s staff team.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Logged data (messages, reports, voice actions) is retained for moderation purposes only. If you wish to request deletion of personal data (such as message logs or avatars), please contact a server administrator or the Bot developer. Requests will be honored to the extent technically feasible.

6. Third-Party Services

The Bot is hosted on Render.com and monitored via UptimeRobot for uptime tracking. These services may collect technical data (such as server status, IP, and uptime statistics) necessary for hosting and monitoring. We do not transmit user data directly to them.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data from unauthorized access or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be reflected in the most recent version distributed with the Bot.

9. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this Policy or your data, please contact the Bot developer through Discord.
